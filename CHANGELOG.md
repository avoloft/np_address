## 1.0.0

* Initial stable release of `np_address` by Avoloft Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
* Complete data for 7 Provinces, 14 Zones, 77 Districts, and 753 Municipalities with English & Nepali Unicode support.
* Type-safe immutable data models: `Province`, `Zone`, `District`, `Municipality`, and `Ward`.
* Fast query and lookup service `NpAddressService` with pre-computed lookup indices.
* Configurable Flutter widget `NpAddressPicker` for hierarchical and standalone dropdowns.
* Comprehensive test suite and zero-dependency core architecture.
